Reliability, safety and security are engineered in, by design and by disciplined, traceable process: isolation, diverse redundancy and independence that no single fault can break, demonstrated with measured evidence, never assumed.

I’m a systems architect and player-coach technical leader for embedded, edge and cloud platforms where reliability, security and predictable performance are acceptance criteria, not aspirations.

My career spans industrial automation and telemetry at NARI; mobile-computing platforms at Qualcomm; platform and firmware security at AMD; OS security hardening and threat detection at BlackBerry; firmware security at NXM Labs; and platform-security delivery, SDV mixed-criticality virtualisation and sovereign AI infrastructure at Linaro. Today I continue with independent open research on Physical AI and agentic-AI accountability.

Track record: Linaro’s first Tier-1 automotive win and an IoT security framework independently certified to PSA Certified Level 2. An early mover in AI-native engineering, I championed Linaro’s company-wide AI/LLM-assisted SDLC strategy and delivered the performance milestones of a fixed-cost engagement at roughly half the effort of comparable past projects. What I’ve built and led

GPU virtualisation for a software-defined vehicle cockpit

Linaro · tech lead and project manager

A safety-relevant instrument cluster and an Android Automotive infotainment guest sharing one Arm SoC and one GPU under a type-1 hypervisor. Led the guest graphics move to gfxstream (Vulkan, GLES via ANGLE, zero-copy rendering over VirtIO-GPU) and benchmark-led optimisation. Met the customer’s virtualisation-overhead target and reached parity with the incumbent stack, and supervised the GPU-arbitration track that keeps the instrument cluster free from interference.

WCET benchmark and outlier attribution for real-time Linux

Linaro · conceived and led

An open-source framework that traces every class of latency outlier in Linux PREEMPT_RT to its root cause; 99.1% of task-switching outliers came from kernel non-preemptibility. It became Linaro’s first automotive benchmark capability and the technical foundation of its first Tier-1 automotive engagement.

Led the end-to-end assessment of an automotive SoC BSP and personally conducted the firmware vulnerability and security gap analysis: secure boot chain (integrity, authenticity, anti-rollback) and cryptographic architecture review, then a UNECE R155/R156 conformance gap analysis with a remediation roadmap.

TEE trusted services for an automotive telematics unit

Hardened TLS with mutual authentication, device-bound secure storage and tamper-resistant logging, built as OP-TEE trusted services for a 5G telematics unit on MediaTek MT2735, toward UN R155 and ISO/SAE 21434 compliance. Delivered and accepted ahead of schedule.

Open research

Current projects

MCIB: Mixed-Criticality Interference Benchmark

An open, platform-neutral specification for attributing interference between AI inference and real-time control on shared silicon: an interference taxonomy, an attribution protocol and a results schema with mandatory provenance. The harness is validated on a Raspberry Pi 4B under PREEMPT_RT; the first measurement campaign is planned on Renesas R-Car V4H.

anchorstamp: attested execution evidence for AI agents

Checks what an AI agent claims it did against what an independent observer on the platform recorded, using signed, hash-chained records. The Linux kernel-audit proof of concept detects forged claims and unreported actions across 11 live adversarial scenarios.

About the mark

Non-deterministic in, predictable out

The orange dots are the non-deterministic world: physical faults, adversaries, the unknown, and now AI inference. Some of them converge through the sky-blue point, where engineering happens: isolation, diverse redundancy, independence and measurement. They resolve into the navy point, which is predictable, bounded behaviour. The rest stay scattered across the field. That is residual risk, acknowledged and managed with evidence rather than wished away. The navy L is the deterministic frame that holds it all, and the axes it is measured against.
