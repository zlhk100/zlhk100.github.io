Where I work

Mixed-criticality and virtualisation

Consolidating safety-relevant and general-purpose workloads onto one SoC under a hypervisor, for software-defined vehicles and industrial systems, then proving they don’t interfere: timing analysis, interference attribution, GPU virtualisation and performance tuning across QNX, AGL, Android Automotive, Gunyah, Xen and KVM.

Full-stack platform security and confidential computing

From hardware root of trust to the cloud: secure and measured boot, TrustZone and TEEs, attestation, device identity and key management, threat modelling and UNECE R155/R156 conformance, through to securing sovereign AI infrastructure.

Physical AI and robotics

Bringing the same reliability and security discipline to robots: measuring how AI inference interferes with real-time control on shared silicon, and what to do about it.

Selected work

What I’ve built and led

GPU virtualisation for a software-defined vehicle cockpit

Linaro · tech lead and project manager

A safety-relevant instrument cluster and an Android Automotive infotainment guest sharing one Arm SoC and one GPU under a type-1 hypervisor. Led the guest graphics move to gfxstream (Vulkan, GLES via ANGLE, zero-copy rendering over VirtIO-GPU) and benchmark-led optimisation. Met the customer’s virtualisation-overhead target and reached parity with the incumbent stack.

WCET benchmark and outlier attribution for real-time Linux

Linaro · conceived and led

An open-source framework that traces every class of latency outlier in Linux PREEMPT_RT to its root cause; 99.1% of task-switching outliers came from kernel non-preemptibility. It became Linaro’s first automotive benchmark capability and the technical foundation of its first Tier-1 automotive engagement.

Open research

Current projects

MCIB: Mixed-Criticality Interference Benchmark

An open, platform-neutral specification for attributing interference between AI inference and real-time control on shared silicon: an interference taxonomy, an attribution protocol and a results schema with mandatory provenance. The harness is validated on a Raspberry Pi 4B under PREEMPT_RT; the first measurement campaign is planned on Renesas R-Car V4H.

Apache-2.0CC BY 4.0PREEMPT_RT
GitHub repository

anchorstamp: attested execution evidence for AI agents

Checks what an AI agent claims it did against what an independent observer on the platform recorded, using signed, hash-chained records. The Linux kernel-audit proof of concept detects forged claims and unreported actions across 11 live adversarial scenarios.
